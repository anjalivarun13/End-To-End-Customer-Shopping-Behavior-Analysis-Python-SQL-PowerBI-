# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📖 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using transactional data from **3,900 purchases** across multiple product categories. The objective is to uncover insights into customer spending patterns, purchasing preferences, subscription behavior, and product performance to support data-driven business decisions.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow using **Python, MySQL, and Power BI**, transforming raw data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, analysis, and interactive visualization.

---

## 📂 Dataset Summary

**Dataset Size**
- Records: 3,900
- Features: 18

**Key Information**
- Customer Demographics (Age, Gender, Location, Subscription Status)
- Purchase Details (Product, Category, Amount, Season, Size, Color)
- Shopping Behavior (Discounts, Purchase Frequency, Reviews, Shipping Type)

**Data Quality**
- Missing values identified in the Review Rating field and addressed during data preparation.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

### 1. Business Understanding
Defined business objectives and key analytical questions related to customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and subscription trends.

### 2. Data Preparation & EDA (Python)
- Loaded and explored the dataset.
- Handled missing values and improved data quality.
- Standardized column names for consistency.
- Created additional features such as customer age groups and purchase frequency metrics.
- Prepared a clean dataset for database integration.

### 3. Data Analysis (MySQL)
Performed business-focused analysis to identify:
- Revenue distribution across customer segments.
- Impact of discounts on purchasing behavior.
- Top-rated and most frequently purchased products.
- Subscriber versus non-subscriber performance.
- Customer loyalty patterns and repeat purchasing behavior.
- Revenue contribution by different age groups.

### 4. Dashboard Development (Power BI)
Connected Power BI with MySQL to build an interactive dashboard for business reporting and decision-making.

## 📈 Key Insights

- Subscribers generated higher overall revenue compared to non-subscribers.
- Repeat customers contributed significantly to total sales.
- Certain products showed strong dependency on discounts for purchases.
- High-rated products consistently performed better across categories.
- Specific age groups contributed a larger share of total revenue.
- Express shipping users demonstrated higher average purchase values.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase subscription adoption through exclusive member benefits.
- Strengthen customer loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.
- Optimize discount strategies to balance revenue growth and profitability.
- Promote top-rated and best-selling products through targeted campaigns.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-value customer segments and age groups.
